# US Analysis Calibration — Design (sub-project 3 of 4)

Date: 2026-10-16 · Status: approved in conversation (approach A), awaiting spec review
Builds on: `2026-10-15-us-core-design.md` (branch `feat/us-core`)

## Goal

Every number the app shows as a measured fact on a US screen is measured on US
data. Today the forecast bands, risk quintiles, regime cutoffs, P/E and dividend
bands and liquidity tiers were all fitted on EGX history and are global
constants. US gets EGX-fitted forecast bands silently, no risk grade at all
(the US liquidity floors are `None`), no Market Condition card, a 400 from
`/api/calibration`, and a PRELIM tag on every score.

Success: with `ENABLED_MARKETS=EG,US`, a US stock page shows a risk grade, a
forecast fitted on US stocks, US-centred valuation reasons, no PRELIM tag; the
US dashboard shows a US Market Condition card; the Calibration screen shows the
US record; EG output is byte-for-byte unchanged.

## User decisions

- Scope: all five surfaces — risk grade + liquidity floors, forecast ranges,
  P/E & dividend bands, liquidity tiers, Market Condition card.
- A measurement that does not hold up ships as honest context with its
  numbers (as EG's regime card does, t=1.74). It is hidden for US only when the
  association has the wrong sign (rho ≤ 0) — a card claiming "broad strength"
  preceded weaker markets would mislead.
- Approach A: one calibration bundle per market.

## Architecture

### 1. `app/core/calibration/` — one bundle per market

```
app/core/calibration/
  __init__.py   # Calibration dataclasses + for_market(market) -> Calibration
  eg.py         # today's EG numbers, moved verbatim (no value changes)
  us.py         # generated by `python -m scripts.calibrate --market US`
```

`Calibration` (frozen dataclass) groups:

- `forecast`: `fitted_at, n_observations, universe_label, sigma_window,
  fit_horizon_days, band_width_pct_of_spot{median,p25,p75}, z_cone{50,80,90,95},
  one_sigma_coverage{ewma{daily,weekly,monthly}, trailing{...}},
  gaussian_cone_90_coverage` (the "textbook band delivered X%" figure, today the
  hardcoded 85.8 in `routers/calibration.py`).
- `regime`: `weak_max, mixed_max, rho, t, n, significant, phase_rhos, bands{weak,
  mixed,broad: median_3m_pct, positive_rate, observations}, universe_indexes,
  show` (`show = rho > 0`).
- `risk`: the `RISK_CALIBRATION` fields + `quintiles` table.
- `fundamentals`: `pe_median, pe_bands` (list of `(upper_bound, points)`),
  `dy_median, dy_bands`. Points stay the EG points; only the BOUNDARIES move,
  so "the median stock scores ≈0" holds on each market.
- `liquidity_tiers`: `{tier: (thin_below, low_below)}` shares/day — replaces
  `indicators._INDEX_LIQ_FLOORS` for that market.

`MarketConfig` keeps `liquid_min_turnover` / `liquid_min_price` (already read by
`/api/risk` and the snapshot cron); US gets the fitted values instead of `None`.
`MarketConfig.score_calibrated` stays as the switch and becomes `True` for US in
the last task, once `us.py` exists.

`for_market(market)` raises for a market without a bundle — no silent EG
fallback. The old module names (`EGX_Z_CONE_60D`, `ONE_SIGMA_COVERAGE_PCT`,
`CALIBRATION`, `REGIME_*`, `RISK_QUINTILES`, `RISK_CALIBRATION`,
`_INDEX_LIQ_FLOORS`) are removed; a guard test fails if any `app/` module defines
or imports them.

### 2. Consumers read the bundle of the market they serve

| consumer | change |
|---|---|
| `forecast.expected_move / outcome_band` | take `cal: Calibration`; `method` becomes `"calibrated_quantile"`; docstrings say "market currency" |
| `routers/analysis.py` | passes `for_market(market)`; forecast disclosure names the market |
| `regime.classify_regime` | takes `cal.regime` |
| `routers/market_regime.py` | drop the EG-only early return; universe = `cal.regime.universe_indexes` members (EG: EGX30+EGX70 from the JSON as today; US: SPX members from `market_universe`); returns `band: None, hidden: true` when `cal.regime.show` is False |
| `risk_grade.grade_universe` + `routers/risk.py` | quintiles and calibration from the bundle |
| `composite.score_quality` | P/E and DY boundaries + "median" wording from `cal.fundamentals`; reason strings name the market's median ("the US median P/E of ~24") |
| `indicators.liquidity_score` | tiers from `cal.liquidity_tiers`; US tiers keyed by index (SPX/NDX/DJI members vs. other listed) |
| `routers/calibration.py` | drop the EG-only 400; build from the bundle; the regime `measured` string and coverage claims come from it |
| `cron.py` risk snapshot | unchanged logic; grades US once floors are set |

`extras_builder` already carries `score_calibrated`; it additionally carries the
market code so composite/indicators can resolve the bundle without new
plumbing through every caller.

### 3. Data pipeline (`scripts/`)

- `backtest.py --market {EG,US}` (default EG, so existing runs are unchanged):
  - cache dir `scripts/.cache` for EG (unchanged), `scripts/.cache/US/` for US;
  - US universe = SPX ∪ NDX ∪ DJI members from `universe.load_universe` (needs
    a DB with a refreshed universe; `--universe-file` accepts a JSON list as an
    offline alternative);
  - each symbol fetched on its resolved exchange (`universe.exchange_map`),
    benchmark `SP:SPX`;
  - dated risk-free from `macro_series` series `US03MY` when present, else the
    run withholds Risk-Adjusted exactly as EG does without a DB;
  - `macro_at`'s `egx30` key becomes `benchmark`.
- `scripts/backfill_macro.py --series US03MY` fetches `TVC:US03MY` daily history
  into `macro_series` (same table and shape as EGINTR), so the backtest grades US
  Risk-Adjusted against the T-bill of its own trailing year.
- `calibrate.py --market {EG,US}`: reads that market's cache; regime universe,
  benchmark, ISIN rule and liquidity floors come from the `MarketConfig` /
  `market_universe`; also fits:
  - **liquidity floors**: US `liquid_min_turnover` = the 5th percentile of
    60-day mean dollar turnover across the US index universe, rounded down to a
    round number; `liquid_min_price` = 5.0 USD (the usual US penny-stock line);
  - **liquidity tiers**: thin/low share-volume thresholds at the same
    percentiles of each tier's volume distribution that EG's NILEX/EGX30
    thresholds sit at in EG's panel;
  - **fundamentals**: from the current `pe_data` rows of the market (a
    snapshot, not a backtest — fundamentals have no history): quartiles of P/E
    and DY; boundaries placed at the same quantiles the EG boundaries sit at in
    EG's distribution.
  - It writes `app/core/calibration/<code>.py` with `--write`, and prints the
    report as today. EG regenerated from an EG cache must reproduce `eg.py`
    within rounding (not run in CI: the cache is not in git).
- Survivorship: the US universe is today's index members. The bundle's
  `universe_label` says so ("current S&P 500 / NASDAQ-100 / Dow 30 members,
  2006–2026") and the Calibration screen shows it.

Runs go in tmux (`egx-api-us-backtest`, log `~/logs/egx-api-us-backtest.log`).

### 4. Mobile

- `PreliminaryTag` / StockCard PRELIM: unchanged code; disappears because the
  API now reports `score_calibrated: true` for US.
- `RiskGradeCard`: copy "across N {market.short_name} stocks", "every tradeable
  {market} stock"; still hidden when `score_calibrated === false` (future markets).
- `MarketRegimeCard`: shown on any market whose response has a band; hidden
  when `hidden: true`; benchmark label from the market ("S&P 500").
- `ForecastCard`: "fitted to how {market adjective} stocks have actually moved".
  Adjective is a new `MarketConfig.adjective` field ("Egyptian", "US") exposed
  by `/api/markets` and `withMarketDefaults`.
- `CalibrationScreen`: copy from the response's `universe_label`; works per
  market.
- Learn content stays Egypt-focused (unchanged ruling from sub-project 2).

### 5. Error handling

- No bundle for a market → `for_market` raises at import of the route's first
  use; covered by a test that every registered market has a bundle.
- US regime universe empty (universe outage) → existing `MIN_SYMBOLS_FOR_REGIME`
  path returns `band: None, stale: true`.
- `pe_data` empty for US when calibrating → calibrate refuses to write
  fundamentals (exit non-zero) rather than writing NaN bands.

## Testing

- EG regression: golden test asserting `eg.py` values equal today's constants
  (copied into the test before the move), and existing EG tests unchanged.
- Guard: no `app/` module defines/imports the removed global names; every
  `MARKETS` entry has a bundle.
- US: risk grade ranks US rows with the fitted floors; regime endpoint returns
  a US band; `/api/calibration?market=US` 200; score_quality gives ≈0 points to
  a stock at the US median P/E and DY; no "EGX"/"Egypt" in US reasons.
- Scripts: `backtest --market US --quick` and `calibrate --market US` on a
  small fixture cache (unit-level, no network).
- Mobile: jest for the market-aware copy; emulator pass on BRK.B (risk grade,
  forecast copy, no PRELIM), US dashboard (Market Condition card), Calibration
  screen in US mode, and an EG spot-check.

## Rollout

Unchanged order from CLAUDE.md, plus: after deploying, re-run
`risk_snapshot?market=US` until every universe symbol has today's row (the new
floors change the tradeable set). The regime reading needs no cron: like EG it
averages the composite scores already cached for the universe (≥15 symbols), so
it appears once the US dashboard has been loaded. Run `backfill_macro --series US03MY` once
against Neon before the first US Risk-Adjusted grading matters (the live app
uses the single current rate; the series only feeds the backtest).

## Out of scope

Dividends, news, macro, alerts, NY-hours crons (sub-project 4); web country
changer; survivorship-free US history; refitting EG.
